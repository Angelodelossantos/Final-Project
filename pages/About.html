Our Story

Founded in 2020, Bean & Brew started with a simple mission: serve the best coffee in town while creating a space for community.


We believe that excellent coffee starts at the source. That's why we travel to remote farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil to find beans that are unique, flavorful, and grown with care.


Our Core Values

  • Quality: Never compromise on bean quality.
  • Community: Support local artists and events.
  • Sustainability: Reduce waste in every cup.
  • Passion: Love what we do, every day.
